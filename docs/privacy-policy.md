# Privacy Policy

**ANYWAVE AI Google Workspace Add-on**
**Effective Date: March 1, 2026**
**Last Updated: March 10, 2026**

ANYWAVE Creations ("we," "us," or "our") operates the ANYWAVE AI Google Workspace Add-on (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Service.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Content Data (Processed In-Memory, Not Stored)

- **Email content**: When you use ANYWAVE AI to draft, summarize, or analyze emails in Gmail, the content of those emails is processed in-memory to generate responses. Email content is **never stored** on our servers and is discarded immediately after processing.
- **Document content**: When you use ANYWAVE AI within Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides, the content of those documents is processed in-memory. Document content is **never stored** on our servers and is discarded immediately after processing.

### 1.2 Data We Store

- **User preferences**: Your selected AI provider, language preferences, feature settings, and add-on configuration are stored to provide a consistent experience across sessions.
- **Usage metrics**: We collect anonymized usage data including request counts, feature usage frequency, error rates, and response times. This data is used to improve the Service and monitor system health.

### 1.3 Account Information

- **Google account identifier**: We store a hashed version of your Google account identifier to manage your subscription tier, usage quotas, and preferences. We do not store your Google password.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
- Process your AI requests through your chosen provider
- Track usage against your subscription tier quota
- Diagnose technical issues and improve reliability
- Communicate important Service updates

## 3. Third-Party AI Services

ANYWAVE AI connects to third-party AI providers to process your requests. You choose which provider to use:

- **Google Gemini** — governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Anthropic Claude** — governed by [Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy)
- **OpenAI** — governed by [OpenAI's Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/privacy)

When you send a request through the Service, the relevant content is transmitted to your selected AI provider for processing. Each provider handles data according to their own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of your chosen provider.

**Important**: We send only the minimum content necessary to fulfill your request. We do not send your account information, preferences, or usage metrics to any AI provider.

## 4. MOBIUS Mesh Processing

When MOBIUS mesh integration is enabled, your data is processed **entirely on your own hardware** within your local network. In this mode:

- No content data leaves your network
- AI processing occurs on your own MOBIUS-connected devices
- No third-party AI services are contacted
- Only anonymized usage counters (request count, not content) are reported back to our servers for quota management

MOBIUS mesh is the most private option available and is recommended for users with sensitive data requirements.

## 5. Data Sharing and Sales

- **We do not sell your personal data to third parties.** This applies to all data categories: content data, preferences, usage metrics, and account information.
- We do not share your data with third parties for their marketing purposes.
- We may share anonymized, aggregated usage statistics that cannot be used to identify any individual user.

### Limited Exceptions

We may disclose information only when:

- Required by law, regulation, or legal process
- Necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of ANYWAVE Creations, our users, or the public
- You have given explicit consent

## 6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

- All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3
- User preferences and metrics are encrypted at rest
- Access to production systems is restricted and audited
- We conduct regular security reviews of our infrastructure

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

- **Content data**: Never stored; discarded immediately after in-memory processing.
- **User preferences**: Retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion or upon request.
- **Usage metrics**: Retained in anonymized form for up to 12 months for service improvement purposes.
- **Account information**: Retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion or upon request.

### Requesting Data Deletion

You may request deletion of all your stored data at any time by:

- Emailing [support@anywavecreations.com](mailto:support@anywavecreations.com) with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
- Including the Google account email address associated with your ANYWAVE AI account

We will process your deletion request within 30 days and confirm completion via email.

## 8. GDPR Compliance (European Economic Area)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Right of access**: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to rectification**: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
- **Right to erasure**: Request deletion of your personal data.
- **Right to restrict processing**: Request that we limit how we use your data.
- **Right to data portability**: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Right to object**: Object to our processing of your personal data.
- **Right to withdraw consent**: Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

Our legal basis for processing personal data is:

- **Legitimate interest**: Usage metrics and service improvement.
- **Contract performance**: Processing necessary to provide the Service you requested.
- **Consent**: Where you have opted into specific features such as third-party AI provider selection.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [support@anywavecreations.com](mailto:support@anywavecreations.com).

## 9. CCPA Compliance (California)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) provides you with the following rights:

- **Right to know**: Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected.
- **Right to delete**: Request deletion of your personal information.
- **Right to opt out of sale**: We do not sell personal information, so this right is satisfied by default.
- **Right to non-discrimination**: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [support@anywavecreations.com](mailto:support@anywavecreations.com).

## 10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete that information promptly.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy within the add-on and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
